new adjusted rules:

for 2 player game:

this game has a new rule such that instead of letting the dice throws be determined by chance,
one person choooses one die and another person chooses the other die..
so you can choose 6 and your opponent choose 2, you both choose at the same time so no one has information until the result is determined.
then you add them up as normal, and you would move 8 squares.


1. Log into chat with username of your choice. leave "password" part empty
2. Push "DICE" button when it is your turn then press 1 or 2 or 3 or 4 or 5 or 6 then wait until opponent does the same.
3. When opponent pushed "DICE' on their turn, you also have to press 1 or 2 or 3 or 4 or 5 or 6
4. When you are in Jail push "JAIL" button instead of "DICE" button then press 1 or 2 or 3 or 4 or 5 or 6
5. To mortgage your property: select "mortgage on menu" then press the property on the map itself
6. To unmortgage your property: select "redeem on menu" then press the property on the map itself
7. press 5/10/25/50 during auction is active. 
8. once you are in jail.  first turn cost = $100  second turn cost = $50  third turn cost = $25  4th turn = free
9. You can mortagage/unmortgage/buyhouse/sellhouse any time you want except during auction
10. landing on treasure-chest =  you gain + $50
11. Clover square = Tax on either property/house/totalbalance
12. 1st Clover = pay property/house at $5
13. 2nd Clover = $5 per house + 5% of your current money
14. 3rd Clover = $10 per non mortgaged property + $5 per mortgaged property
15. for 2-player game only: if you hit 3 doubles in a row: you have option to goto jail or pay $25 to keep rolling again
16. Landing on "parking" gives you option to get free loan of $100 which you need to payback slowly over time
17. Landing on 62 (inner) give you option to run auction on any unowned property of your choice.
