For 2-Player Game only:

This game uses a special dice-selection system instead of relying entirely on random dice rolls.
Each player independently chooses one die from 1 to 6. Both players make their selections at the same time, so neither player knows which number the other player has chosen before the selections are revealed.
For example, if you choose 6 and your opponent chooses 2, the two dice are added together:
6 + 2 = 8
The player then moves 8 spaces, following the normal game rules.
Because both players choose their dice simultaneously, neither player can use the other player's selection as information when making their own choice.


For 3-6 Player Game:

1. Log in to the chat using a username of your choice. Leave the password field empty.
2. Type the !start command. Details are provided at the bottom.
3. When it is your turn, press the DICE button.
4. When you are in Jail, press the JAIL button instead of the DICE button.
5. Jail payment costs are based on the number of turns spent in Jail. If you want to get out early before the 4th turn, press the KEY button.
   1st turn: $100
   2nd turn: $50
   3rd turn: $25
   4th turn: Free
6. During an active auction, use the 5, 10, 25, or 50 buttons to increase your bid.
7. To mortgage a property, select Mortgage from the menu, then select the property directly on the map.
8. To redeem a mortgaged property, select Redeem from the menu, then select the property directly on the map.
9. Properties may be mortgaged or redeemed, and houses may be purchased or sold, at any time except during an active auction.
10. Landing on a Treasure Chest space awards the player $50.
11. Landing on a Clover space activates a tax based on properties, houses, or the player's total balance.
12. 1st Clover: Pay $5 for each property and house.
13. 2nd Clover: Pay $5 for each house plus 5% of your current balance.
14. 3rd Clover: Pay $10 for each non-mortgaged property and $5 for each mortgaged property.
15. Landing on the Parking space awards the player a $100 loan. The loan must be repaid gradually over time.
16. Landing on space 62 in the inner board gives the player the option to start an auction for any unowned property of their choice.

Start Game:
!start <number of players> <deep/regular> <amount>
Examples:  !start 6 deep 1400 | !start 4 regular 1200

Commands that needs to run during a game:
!auction 39 (manually start auction on china)
!switch p1 p2 (used for space-54)
!remove p1 (Removes Player 1 from the current game. Use this when a player has gone bankrupt or is AFK.)
!insert p4 700  (insert player4 in the middle of game with a starting balance of $700)

Trading:
!offer-p1 5,15,25,35 money:25 p2 39 money:1000  -->  p1 offers 5,15,25,35 (4 railroads) + $25  for p2's $1000 and china
!accept
!reject

set Display-name for All 6 Players
Example:  !display MC1 AMAHRIA2 AB3 SARAF4 TODO5 CRINJAL6 
Example:  !display Mary1 John2 Barb3 Will4 p5 p6

rename to custom name (2 letters + 1 number only)
Example: !rename p1 AL1 | !rename p2 ED2

Rarely used commands:
!diceoverride (use this command to override someone's turn)
!gobonus p2 50 250 (bank owes you total of $250 which will be paid everytime you pass go of 50 x 5)
!save (save game)
!status (example:  !status p1)
